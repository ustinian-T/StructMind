# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Students preparing for a data-structures final exam. They use the product in focused study sessions, usually on a laptop or phone, and need fast practice against the real question bank without wondering whether a question or answer was changed.

## Product Purpose

This product turns the Word exam-training bank and assignment bank into a local practice system. It supports ordered practice, random practice, AI-generated variations, discussion-question feedback, assignment-question review, answer checking, wrong-question review, and import auditing. Success means the imported question count and answer data are traceable, objective questions are graded against the source answer key, assignment answers keep their source labels, and AI features never replace the source bank as the truth for ordered or random practice.

## Brand Personality

Clear, calm, precise. The interface should feel like a quiet study desk with visible progress and immediate feedback, not a marketing site or game dashboard.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic education landing pages, oversized hero sections, loud gamification, heavy gradients, and decorative motion. Avoid any UI that hides the imported-data audit or makes AI-generated questions look equivalent to the official source bank.

## Design Principles

- Keep the question as the center of the screen.
- Make source-bank trust visible through counts, audit notes, and answer provenance.
- Separate official-bank grading, Word assignment answers, and AI reference answers.
- Reward steady practice with progress and review loops, not visual noise.
- Let long study sessions stay readable on desktop and mobile.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for all text and controls. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, readable Chinese text, mobile layouts, and `prefers-reduced-motion` for users who reduce animation at the system level.
